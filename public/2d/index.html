      How to play

      Your ring has a gap. It always faces the way you steer. Drive into the orb and it drops into your ring. Keep moving and you carry it.

      Dash fires it. Dashing slams the back of your ring into the orb for a power shot. Stop short and the orb rolls out the front as a pass.

      Dash into an opponent to knock them loose. A hit ring goes hollow for half a second and drops the orb. You hold two dashes; each refills in 1.4 seconds.

      The buzzer waits for the orb. At 0:00, play goes on while someone holds the orb in their ring or it's inside a colored goal zone. The match ends once it's loose on white ice. A goal that ties it sends the game to overtime.

      Mastery lives in the spin. Whip your ring around and the edge of the gap flicks the orb. Bank it off the boards. Read the bounce.
