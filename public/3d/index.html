      How to play

      Your ring has a gap. It faces the way you steer. Drive into the orb and it drops into your ring. Keep moving to carry it.

      Dash fires it. Dashing slams the back of your ring into the orb for a rising power shot. You hold two dashes; each refills in 1.4 seconds. Dash into an opponent to knock them loose.

      Kickoffs start dash-free. After each kickoff, dashes unlock the moment anyone touches the orb, or after 3 seconds.

      Hop to play the air. Hop once off the ice, and once more in mid-air. Hop while carrying to scoop the orb up with you, then dash into it for an aerial shot. Watch the orb’s shadow to read where it lands.

      Ball cam keeps the orb in view. Pushing forward always drives toward the orb. Turn it off to have the camera follow your ring instead.

      The buzzer waits for the orb. At 0:00 play goes on while the orb is in a ring, in the air, or in a colored goal zone.

      Every goal gets a replay, slowed down at the shot and at the goal line. Tap or press any button to skip; it ends once every player has skipped.
